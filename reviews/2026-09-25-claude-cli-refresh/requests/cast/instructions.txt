Describe the kind of clothes the character typically wears but not an individual outfit, let the image generation AI choose that. Format all descriptive text fields (character descriptions, backstories and relationships) using Markdown: use **bold** for emphasis and important moments, *italics* for atmosphere and inner thoughts, and blank lines to separate paragraphs.
